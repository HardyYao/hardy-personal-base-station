## 一次糟糕的打车经历

昨晚十点到深圳北站，首次尝试在深圳北站打车回公寓。

深圳北站在西广场负一楼设置了专门的网约车A车和B区（地面上禁止司机接单，据说违反规定的要交1千块钱罚单），A区离出站口比较近，我就在A区打车。

这次打车体验非常糟糕。

首先是叫车非常难，原本 40 元左右的行程，我一路把价格加到了 70+ 的轻享车规格，才有司机接单。

其次是通道非常拥堵，几百米的距离，司机开了将近十分钟才到达接送地点（这也是很多司机不太愿意接单的主要原因）。

最后，也是最糟糕的一点，司机如果在网约车区停留超过十分钟，就要交十块钱费用，而这部分费用经常引起司机和乘客之间的矛盾，毕竟谁也不想出这个钱。

我运气不错，司机速度比较快，不用交这个钱，也就不用我出钱了。

不过司机在走出拥堵路段期间，还是多次跟我吐槽了这个事情，觉得这种规划太不合理了，不让司机在宽敞的地面接单，非要把他们赶到拥堵的地下停车区域，停留超过十分钟还要交钱，服务意识太糟糕了。

前两年，有一次回老家，因为出发时间比较早，地铁还没发车，我就直接打车到深圳北站。所以我其实知道，深圳北站东广场那边出去的天桥对面，网约车司机是可以停车、接客的，但我昨晚没有走那条路线，因为我想看看，在深圳北站专门给大家规划的网约车区域叫车，是怎样一种体验。

可惜的是，体验非常糟糕，我以后再也不会去深圳北站西广场打车了。

我之前一直觉得深圳北站挺光鲜亮丽的，没想到这次突发奇想的打车经历，让我看到了它非常糟糕的一面。

## A Bad Taxi Experience

Last night at around 10 p.m., I arrived at Shenzhen North Railway Station and tried to take a ride back to my apartment for the first time.

At the station, there are special pick-up areas for online ride-hailing cars (called Zone A and Zone B) on the B1 floor of the West Square.

Drivers are not allowed to pick up passengers on the ground level — if they do, they can be fined 1,000 yuan.

Zone A is closer to the exit, so I decided to take a car there.

Unfortunately, the whole experience was terrible.

First, it was really hard to get a ride.

Normally, the trip costs around 40 yuan, but I had to raise the price again and again — up to more than 70 yuan for a “Comfort” car — before a driver finally accepted my order.

Second, the traffic in the underground area was extremely bad.

The driver took almost ten minutes to drive just a few hundred meters to the pick-up spot.

This is probably the main reason why many drivers don’t want to take orders from this area.

And the worst part is:

If a driver stays in the ride-hailing zone for more than ten minutes, they have to pay a 10-yuan parking fee.

This rule often causes arguments between drivers and passengers because no one wants to pay it.

Luckily, my driver was fast enough, so we didn’t have to pay the extra fee.

However, while we were driving out of the crowded area, the driver kept complaining about the station’s bad design.

He said it was unreasonable that drivers couldn’t pick up passengers on the open ground level, and instead were forced into the crowded underground area — and even had to pay money if they stayed too long.

He felt the service planning was really poor.

A few years ago, when I went back to my hometown, I also took a taxi to Shenzhen North Station early in the morning before the subway started running.

So I actually knew that on the East Square side, across the overpass, online drivers can still stop and pick up passengers.

But last night, I didn’t take that route.

I wanted to experience what it’s like to call a car from the “officially planned” ride-hailing area.

Unfortunately, the experience was really bad.

I’ll never take a car from the West Square of Shenzhen North Station again.

I used to think Shenzhen North Station looked modern and impressive,but this unexpected experience showed me a very unpleasant side of it.
