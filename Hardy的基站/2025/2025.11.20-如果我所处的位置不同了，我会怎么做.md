# 如果我所处的位置不同了，我会怎么做

前两周跟朋友见面时，聊到一个话题，我说，我很反感很多公司高层领导不把底下员工当人看的残忍行为。

朋友问题：如果是你处在那个位置，你会怎么做？

我一时也答不上来，虽然很想直接说，自己不可能会那么无情，但毕竟也不是个未经世事的少年了，知道该对自己说出的话负责任，因此也没有夸下海口。

而是从自己的当下说起，我说，至少，我从一个普通职员升上小组长之后，跟自己的组员相处得还是比较好的，也会帮他们扛一些压力，会请他们吃个饭啥的。

但我自己也知道，这个回答是不够的。

这几天，我有了一个初步的答案：

如果我真的站在那个位置，并且具备一定的话语权了，我会尽一切努力去把蛋糕分配得更加平均一些，让底层员工多得到一些好处，而不仅仅是给一些毫无实际用处的口头表扬。

我会尽力去做好这件事，如果最终发现，我改变不了那个环境，那我会选择主动退出高层，离开那个环境。

以上，就是最接近现阶段的我的一个想法。

现阶段的我，本身也没那么强烈的向上攀升欲望，只想过好自己的小日子。如果有能力了，也还是希望创造出一个更好的环境来，而把自己当人看、把别人当人看，就是创造一个好环境的要素之一。

这是现在的我，能够给出的答案。

## If I Were in a Different Position, What Would I Do?

Two weeks ago, when I was meeting a friend, we talked about a topic:

I said I really dislike how many top leaders in companies treat their employees without respect, almost as if they are not human.

My friend asked me:

“If you were the one in that position, what would you do?”

At that moment, I couldn’t answer directly.

Although I wanted to say, “I would never be that cold-hearted,”

I’m not a naïve teenager anymore — I know I should be responsible for what I say.

So I didn’t make any promises.

Instead, I started from where I am now.

I said that after I was promoted from a regular employee to a team leader,

I’ve tried my best to get along well with my team members.

I help them take on some pressure, and sometimes I treat them to dinner.

But I also knew that this answer wasn’t enough.

---

### My Thought After a Few Days

Over the past few days, I found a more complete answer:

If I were truly in that high-level position and had real influence,I would try my best to distribute benefits more fairly.

I would make sure that lower-level employees get more real advantages,

not just empty praise that means nothing.

I would do everything I could.

But if, in the end, I realized that I couldn’t change the environment,

then I would choose to step down voluntarily and leave that place.

That is the honest answer from who I am at this stage.

---

### What I Want at This Stage

Right now, I don’t have a strong desire to climb upward in the hierarchy.

I just want to live a good, simple life.

If I do have ability in the future,

I hope to create a better environment for others too.

Seeing myself as a human being,

and seeing others as human beings —

this is one of the basic elements of creating a good environment.

This is the answer I can give today.
