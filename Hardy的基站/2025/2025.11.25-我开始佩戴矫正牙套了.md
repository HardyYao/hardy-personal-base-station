昨天晚上，开始佩戴我的第一副矫正牙套了，接下来就是十分漫长的矫正过程了。

刚算了一下，从 2021 年到现在，不算今年牙齿矫正的费用，我在处理牙齿问题（补牙、窝沟封闭和根管治疗等）上的花费，都已经比我一年的房租还要多了。

回顾一下自己补牙的几次经历：

1、2018年年初，在大学毕业前的寒假，我有两颗牙齿蛀牙了，过年回老家后，就去老家那边的牙科补牙，收费很便宜，但是没有补好。对比起我这几年常去的这家牙科，当时老家的那家牙科，其实相当于完全没有给我补牙，最直观的体验就是，在那边补完牙之后，我每次喝水的时候，牙齿都会觉得有点疼，而我当时那两颗牙齿，牙根应该还是好的，并不难补，只能说，没去对地方。

不过那时候我并不了解这些事情，也不怎么在意。

2、2020年，还是那两颗牙齿，再次蛀牙，而且这次疼得更厉害，但我还是不够重视，以为再去补一下就好了。后面去深圳一家医院补的牙，有一颗补得还行、还有一颗补得很差（当时不知道，以为都补得很好，到后面再次蛀牙才发现这个问题）。

3、2021年，还是那两颗牙齿，这次有一颗牙齿的牙根都蛀坏掉了，五一假期期间，疼得我晚上经常睡不着觉，那个时候还不知道是牙根坏了，以为只是上火导致牙龈疼痛。

假期结束后，去深圳一家正规的牙科诊所洗牙。

这次洗牙，除了清理掉了一大堆牙结石，还发现自己将近一半的牙齿都有大大小小的问题。

后面将近两个月时间里，修复了十来颗有问题的牙齿（五六颗做了窝沟封闭，还有几颗蛀牙蛀的不厉害、简单补了一下），其中有一颗牙齿做了根管治疗，耗时将近一个月。另外还有两颗牙齿，因为考虑后期可能要做矫正，就暂时搁置处理。

去完这家诊所之后，我总算开始养成了饭后用牙线、注意口腔卫生的好习惯。

在2021年之前，由于大量的牙结石，导致我刷牙时经常牙龈出血，多年来我也一直都没有把这个当成严重的问题来看待，直到清理掉牙结石之后，才知道拥有不会牙龈肿痛、不会牙龈出血的牙齿是多么幸福的一件事。

4、2023年，再次去那家牙科洗牙，顺便检查一下牙齿问题。2022年，由于众所周知的原因，出行很不方便，所以我一直没有去做矫正，到2023年也没有这个想法了，就直接把之前遗留有问题的牙齿都给补上了。

5、今年，去洗牙的时候，发现又有两颗蛀牙。补牙期间，我开始重新考虑矫正的事情。

先前暂时搁置了矫正的想法，一方面是对费用有所顾虑（虽然当时的我也不差这笔钱，但还是会有点心疼），另一方面则是觉得太麻烦，觉得矫正时间太长，影响生活（认知水平不够，没有考虑到这件事所带来的长远效益）。

本来我是想，把该补的牙都补完了，加上自己已经养成了饭后漱口和使用牙线的习惯，我的牙齿应该也不会有多大的问题了。

结果，2023 年和 2024 年去复查还有洗牙的时候，我的牙齿情况的确还可以，但是今年又有蛀牙了。

主要还是因为我的牙齿太不整齐了，不只是刷牙有点困难，平时的咬合对牙齿的磨损也比较大。

对于一些人来说，矫正或许只是为了更加美观，但对于我来说，矫正则事关我左边牙齿的牙根问题。前几个月牙科诊所去拍了片，医生跟我说，因为我左边牙齿存在比较严重的错位问题，导致无法正常咬合，长期下来，还会导致牙根萎缩，我现在左边的好几颗牙齿，牙根其实已经坏掉一部分了，如果持续下去，情况只会更糟糕。

我后面也去查了一下资料，确实存在这种情况，而且我自己非常清楚我左边的牙齿到底不整齐到了什么程度。

我之前一直觉得，不矫正最多只是说不太美观而已，所以也没有特别在意。而现在，则事关牙根问题了。考虑了一段时间后，我决定，不再犹豫了，今年一定要去矫正。

之前有不少人跟我说过，牙齿矫正一定要在多少岁之前完成比较好，如果过了那个年龄段，就不要再去矫正了。的确，牙科医生也跟我说，如果我在成年之前就去正畸了，那我现在牙齿的状况肯定会好很多，可那对我来说已经是不可能的事情了。

我能改变的，只有未来，至少我很确定，今年去矫正，总比明年再去矫正要好得多。

我最近在想，如果我从小接受的教育，能够让我学会关注自己的身体和心理健康，那我是不是早就已经处理好了我的牙齿问题了？

可惜人生没有如果，我所接受的教育，让我在很长的一段时间里，都觉得，我自己的身体和感受并不重要，很多事情，我都是能忍则忍。

就是因为太能忍了，我才会忍到2021年、第一次洗完牙之后才知道，因为牙结石过多而经常牙龈出血是多么严重的一个问题，我其实应该在成年前就要知道，这是不正常的，我必须得去处理好这个问题。而我处理好这个问题的那一年，我已经26岁了。

我也很庆幸，2021年的时候，我找对了牙科诊所，这家牙科收费确实相对来说会贵一点，但是他们家的环境、服务以及补牙所用的材料真的是特别、特别好。

我有一个大学同学，前几年也去做了根管治疗，他好像是去三甲医院，然后走医保报销了很多钱。前两年，我一个同事听说我的牙齿问题后，也跟我说，我其实可以去三甲医院做根管治疗，那样可以报销，基本都不用花钱。

但我不这样认为，因为我那个在三甲医院算是“免费”做了根管治疗的同学，他说他牙齿补完后，都不能咬硬东西了。而我2021年在我现在常去的这家牙科诊所补的牙齿，特别好用，我每天照样可以啃大鱼大肉，从来没有觉得稍微咬点硬东西牙齿就要坏掉了。

有些钱，该花就得花，换来的回报是相当丰厚的。

毕竟，对于普通人来说，除了阳光、空气和水，应该不会有什么免费的好东西能落到自己头上。

记录一下自己的这段经历，也感谢自己：这几年来，越来越懂得关注自己的身体和心理健康了。

## I Started Wearing My Orthodontic Aligners

Last night, I started wearing my first set of orthodontic aligners.

This marks the beginning of a very long treatment process.

I just calculated that from 2021 until now — not including this year’s orthodontic cost — the money I spent on dental problems (fillings, fissure sealants, root canal treatment, etc.) is already more than my yearly rent.

---

### Looking Back at My Dental Treatment Experiences

1. In early 2018, before graduating from university, I had two decayed teeth.

I went to a local dental clinic in my hometown to fix them.

The price was cheap, but the treatment was not good.

Compared with the clinic I visit nowadays, my teeth were basically not fixed at all.

After that treatment, even drinking water made my teeth hurt.

Those two teeth were not hard to fix at that time, so the problem was simply that I went to the wrong place.

But I didn’t understand anything back then and didn’t care much.

---

2. In 2020, the same two teeth decayed again, and the pain became worse.

I still didn’t take it seriously and thought another filling would fix everything.

I went to a hospital in Shenzhen—one tooth was fixed okay, but the other was poorly treated.

I didn’t know it at the time. I only realized it when the tooth decayed again later.

---

3. In 2021, the same two teeth had problems again.

One of them already had a damaged root.

During the May Day holiday, the pain kept me awake at night.

I didn’t know the root was damaged; I thought it was gum inflammation.

After the holiday, I went to a professional dental clinic in Shenzhen for a cleaning.

Besides removing a huge amount of tartar,

the dentist told me that almost half of my teeth had different levels of problems.

I spent almost two months fixing everything:

5–6 teeth got fissure sealants, a few small cavities were filled, and one tooth needed a root canal, which took almost a full month.

Two teeth were left untreated because I might need orthodontics later.

After visiting this clinic, I finally developed the habit of using dental floss after meals and paying attention to oral hygiene.

Before 2021, due to excessive tartar, my gums often bled when brushing.

For many years, I didn’t treat it like a serious issue.

Only after cleaning the tartar did I realize how happy it is to have teeth that don’t hurt or bleed.

---

4. In 2023, I went back for cleaning again and fixed the teeth we left untreated in 2021.

Because travel was inconvenient in 2022, I delayed orthodontics.

By 2023, I didn’t think much about it anymore — I just fixed everything first.

---

5. This year, during another cleaning, I found two new cavities.

While fixing them, I started thinking about orthodontics again.

---

### Why I Delayed Orthodontics Before

I put off the idea for two reasons:

1. Cost

— even though I could afford it, the amount still made me hesitate.

2. Trouble & time

— I felt orthodontics took too long and would affect my life.

(Back then, I didn’t understand the long-term benefits.)

I thought that once all the cavities were fixed,

and with my flossing habit, my teeth wouldn’t have big problems anymore.

But during my check-ups in 2023 and 2024, even though my overall condition was okay,

I still found new cavities this year.

The main reason is that my teeth are too crowded and misaligned.

It’s hard to brush properly, and my bite puts extra wear on some teeth.

For some people, orthodontics is for appearance.

But for me, it’s about protecting the roots of my left teeth.

A few months ago, the dentist told me that because several teeth on my left side are seriously misaligned,

I can’t bite properly.

Over time, this causes root resorption.

Some of my left teeth already have partial root damage.

If I don’t treat it, things will only get worse.

I later searched for information, and it’s true.

And I know very well how crooked my left teeth are.

I used to think orthodontics was only about looks,

so I didn’t care.

But now it’s about tooth roots — a much bigger issue.

After thinking about it, I decided to stop hesitating:

I must get orthodontic treatment this year.

---

### “Isn’t It Too Late to Start Orthodontics?”

Many people told me orthodontic treatment should be done at a young age.

If you’re older, you shouldn’t do it anymore.

Dentists also told me that if I had corrected my teeth before adulthood,

my dental condition would be much better now.

But that’s already impossible.

What I can change is my future.

Starting this year is definitely better than starting next year.

---

### Reflecting on My Past

Recently, I’ve been wondering:

If my education had taught me to pay attention to my physical and mental health earlier,

wouldn’t I have solved my dental problems long ago?

But life doesn’t have “what if.”

For a long time, my education made me believe that my body and feelings were not important —

I just endured everything silently.

Because I endured too much,

I didn’t realize until 2021 — after my first professional cleaning —

how serious gum bleeding caused by tartar actually was.

I should have known this before adulthood.

But by the time I fixed it, I was already 26.

I am thankful that in 2021, I finally found a good clinic.

Although their prices are higher,

their environment, service, and materials are extremely good.

One of my college classmates had a root canal in a major public hospital,

and most of the cost was covered by insurance.

A coworker also told me I could save money by going to a big hospital.

But I disagree.

That classmate said he couldn’t bite hard food after his treatment.

Meanwhile, the tooth I treated in 2021 works perfectly —

I can eat whatever I want.

Some money is worth spending.

The return is priceless.

For ordinary people, besides sunlight, air, and water,

there’s nothing “good” that comes for free.

---

### In the End

I wanted to record this part of my life

and also thank myself —

because in the past few years,

I’ve learned to care more about my physical and mental health.
