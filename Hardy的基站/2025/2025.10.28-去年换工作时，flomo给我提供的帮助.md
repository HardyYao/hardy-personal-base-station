在去年换工作时，flomo给我提供的帮助，主要包含以下三点：

1.我在使用每日回顾功能回顾之前的笔记时，发现自己但凡记录到跟前公司有关的事情，都不是什么正向的内容。

我从中意识到，继续留在那个地方，只会不断地消耗掉我的心力，让我失去原有的朝气与活力，所以我下定决心要离开那里。

2、决定要从上家公司辞职后，我就对自己提出一个很关键的问题：下一份工作，我想要找什么类型的？

某天，我无意间在 flomo 翻到半年多以前的一条笔记：我的下一份工作，要找跟制造业相关的开发工作。

然后我回顾了自己以往的工作经历，发现除了上一份工作，我的其它工作经历，都是跟制造业相关的。

我似乎也挺适合去开发制造业相关的系统，这类系统，往往对于技术并没有太高要求，但是很重视业务流程。而梳理清楚业务逻辑、输出业务流程图和业务说明文档，刚好都是我比较擅长的事情，也是我比较喜欢做的事情。

熟悉了制造业的业务知识，将来就算不做开发了，转型去做业务专家、去做方案专家或者是其他管理岗位，也都是有机会的。

所以，我希望自己的下一份工作，要尽量往制造业方向靠拢。

最终，关于这个问题，我得出的结论大致如下：

2.1、最优选项：找一份开发制造业信息管理系统相关的新工作，并且所在团队要很有发展前景，也能够给出比较有竞争力的薪资待遇。

2.2、普通选项：如果实在找不到制造业的岗位，也可以继续在互联网行业找，但不要是外包公司，可以是有发展潜力的初创公司，也可以是较为成熟的中大型公司，关键是要有稳定的项目或产品可以做。

2.3、最后选项：如果以上两种都找不到，我允许自己有几个月的职场空窗期，一边找工作，一边修养身心，但是一旦超过一定时间范围，就不要挑了，外包什么的也行，先有份工作再说。

3、想清楚要找什么类型的工作之后，我就开始采取行动，刷面试题、在招聘软件寻找合适的岗位、投简历。

在这个过程中，我经常在 flomo 里面记录自己找工作的相关经历，并且一直在给自己加油打气。

那段时间，只要一打开 flomo，翻看一下自己记录的笔记，我就会觉得自己是个能量充沛的人，这对我找工作也提供了很大的帮助。

我当时在flomo里面记录过一条笔记，其中有一段的内容如下：

此刻我的心境：积极向上、充满自信，对生活充满热爱，不害怕困难，相信自己有能力解决各种问题，能够源源不断给自己提供心力。

那段经历，也给了我一个很好的启发：很多时候，我们做成某件事情的关键，就是勇敢地迈出第一步，然后坚持下去，而在这个过程中，我们并不需要使用多么高大上的技巧和方法论，相反，我们只需要给自己提供源源不断的心力，让自己有足够的能量坚持下去就好了。

而给自己加油打气，让自己的精神状态处于一个正向的环境当中，就是最为关键的一个要素。

很幸运，最终，我的新工作基本是符合最优选项的。

虽然不是什么知名大厂，但是工作内容是我比较感兴趣的，收入尚可，工作压力也不大，也不用被迫去卷各种没有意义的事情。

有事情可以做，也有自己的时间可以去体验生活，我很喜欢目前的工作和生活状态。

## How Flomo Helped Me When I Changed Jobs Last Year

When I changed jobs last year, Flomo helped me a lot in three main ways:

### 1. Understanding My True Feelings About My Old Job

When I used Flomo’s Daily Review feature to look back at my past notes,

I noticed that every time I wrote something about my previous company, it was never positive.

From that, I realized that staying there would only keep draining my energy and make me lose my motivation and passion.

So, I decided it was time to leave.

### 2. Figuring Out What Kind of Job I Wanted Next

After deciding to quit, I asked myself a key question:

“What kind of job do I want next?”

One day, I accidentally found a note I had written more than half a year earlier in Flomo.

It said: “My next job should be a development job related to the manufacturing industry.”

Then I reviewed my work experience and found that, except for my last job, all my previous jobs were related to manufacturing.

I realized that I might actually be quite suitable for developing systems for the manufacturing industry.

These systems usually don’t require very advanced technology, but they place great importance on business processes.

And organizing business logic, drawing process flowcharts, and writing documentation are exactly what I’m good at — and enjoy doing.

Once I become familiar with manufacturing business knowledge, even if I stop working as a developer one day,

I can still move into roles like business expert, solution specialist, or even management positions.

So, I decided that my next job should move in the direction of manufacturing.

### My Conclusions

After thinking carefully, I came to three possible options:

2.1 Best option:

Find a new job developing manufacturing information management systems — in a team with good growth potential and competitive pay.

2.2 Normal option:

If I can’t find a manufacturing-related job, I can stay in the internet industry —

but not in an outsourcing company.

It could be a promising startup or a stable medium-to-large company,

as long as there are real, long-term projects to work on.

2.3 Last option:

If neither of the first two options works out, I’ll allow myself a few months off.

During that time, I’ll rest and look for new opportunities.

But if the break gets too long, I’ll take any job I can — even outsourcing —

just to stay active in the workplace.

### 3. Staying Positive During My Job Search

Once I was clear about what kind of job I wanted, I began to take action —

I practiced interview questions, searched job apps for suitable positions, and sent out resumes.

During this process, I often used Flomo to record my job-hunting experiences and to encourage myself.

At that time, whenever I opened Flomo and read my notes,

I could feel my energy returning.

It helped me a lot to stay motivated while job hunting.

I once wrote this note in Flomo:

My current state of mind: Positive, confident, full of love for life, not afraid of difficulties, and believing that I have the ability to solve problems — I can always give myself the inner strength I need.

That experience taught me something important:

Many times, the key to success is simply to take the first step and keep going.

We don’t need fancy methods or big theories —

we just need to keep giving ourselves positive energy and enough strength to continue.

Encouraging ourselves and staying in a positive mental environment

is one of the most important parts of any journey.

### The Result

In the end, the new job I found matched my best option quite well.

It’s not a famous big company,

but the work is interesting, the pay is decent,

and the pressure is not high.

I don’t have to take part in meaningless internal competition anymore.

Now, I have meaningful work to do,

and I also have enough personal time to enjoy life.

I truly like my current job and lifestyle.
