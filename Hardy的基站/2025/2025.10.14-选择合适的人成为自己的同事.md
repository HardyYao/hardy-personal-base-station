今晚跟部门的同事（下文简称为A）在办公室闲聊了3个多小时才下班，从找对象、结婚、对买车和买房的观点、再到职场中的工作沟通、公司发展的各种话题都聊了一遍。

聊完之后下班回家路上，我回想起当初招聘的一些事：

前几个月招人的时候，我看了几百份简历，面试了十几、二十个Java开发，最终在7月底的时候才看到两份比较合适的简历，其中一个就是A。当时我先约了A第二天线上面试，另一个人（下文简称为B）则是约的第二天下午线下面试。

当天上午和A线上面试完之后，我回到办公室跟我导师还有其他同事说，我觉得A非常不错，再看看下午那个B，但我觉得A完全能够符合我们的工作要求了，我觉得他的沟通理解能力很强，技术水平也挺不错。

当天下午，B来面试了。比较出乎意料的是，原本从简历上来看，B应该比我A更契合我们要求，他的工作经历跟我们目前所开发系统的业务需求更契合。

但是面试的时候，B给我一种一问三不知的感觉。

技术方面，我是从一些最简单的问题开始问，他一个都答不上来，他说他还没有开始准备刷题。但我觉得作为一个有过好几年工作经验的Java开发，就算再怎么没准备，我前面问的一些这么基础的问题也不应该都不懂，首先这一点就让我对他的印象大打折扣。

业务方面，他的回答也跟技术相关问题的水平差不多，基本没有什么能够回答上来的，讲的都是一些零零散散的东西。

之后我导师和B现场面了一下，我导师对他的评价是：基本也能派得上用场，但是可能我需要花比较多的时间去带他。

我则回复我导师，我想约A来线下面试一下，我更看好他。

隔天，A过来现场面试了，最终他也通过了我们这边的面试，成为了我的新同事。

我觉得人的直觉还是蛮准的，当初面试的时候，我就觉得我跟A应该会很聊得来。果然，他入职之后，我们很快就成为了关系比较要好的同事。

我们部门其实还有另一个安卓开发的同事，但是我跟他认识一年多了，却从没有过比较愉快的日常交流。第一个原因是那个安卓开发同事比较闷，第二点则是他的沟通理解能力相对来说挺糟糕的。在工作当中，因为他的沟通和理解能力比较差，也给我造成过不少困扰。

A在性格和沟通理解能力方面跟我比较接近，我们都属于相对内向的人，但只要遇到同频的人，交流起来就挺愉快的。他精神面貌也满不错，平时喜欢去健身房撸铁。我近几个月刚好有想要加强这一块锻炼的计划，最近在他的帮助下已经开始进行一些有效锻炼了，后面还打算办一张和他同一家健身房的健身卡，请他帮我指导一下。

今天他跟我聊到，他已经入职两个月，下个月差不多到转正时间了，还问了我关于他转正后薪资的问题。我跟他说，上次他跟我提过诉求之后，我在国庆前已经跟我们部长反馈过了，我肯定会尽量帮他争取到期望的转正薪资。

我也跟部长他们说过，他能力很强，不管是技术还是沟通理解能力都非常出色，是一位难得的好同事。我希望公司能给他比较好的待遇，让他工作得更开心。我还会跟另一位领导转达他的诉求，这是我作为小组长应该为他做的事情，能争取的福利我一定尽力帮他争取。

A入职后，确实给我减轻了不少压力。原先我一个人面对下半年繁重的开发任务，还要兼顾其他几个子系统的日常维护工作，有时真的感到有点力不从心。但A技术水平在某些方面甚至比我更出色，业务理解能力也很强，简单跟他讲一下，他基本都能很快上手，快速开发出相应的功能接口。

原先我们计划要到11月中下旬才开发完全部功能接口，但按照目前的进度，我跟他再赶一赶，快的话下周就可以把全部功能接口提前完成，然后逐步完善，这比我们原先预期的要快很多。所以最近几周我们俩都感觉越来越轻松了，加班也比之前少了。

最近这段时间，除了工作、看书和写作，我每天都会花一个多小时的时间学习英语，包括听英语音频、看英语视频、练口语。

有了自己想要沉浸其中的事情之后，其实最近两个月，我线上线下的社交活动已经减少了很多了，因为想花更多时间和精力在自己感兴趣的事情上面。

今天晚上我本来也打算早早回家，看书、写点文字，再学习一下语言，但在办公室跟A聊得很愉快，就觉得改变一下计划也无妨。

去年来到这家新公司后，虽然工作比较轻松，但我跟另一个开发同事不太聊得来，其他部门的同事跟我工作又没有交集，所以我已经有一年多没有跟职场的同事有过愉快的日常交流了。

A则给我一种很想和他成为好朋友的感觉，尤其今晚跟他的这次交流，让我有一种当初招对了人的感觉，很开心自己当时选了A作为自己的新同事。

## Choosing the Right Person to Be Your Colleague

Tonight, I chatted with one of my department colleagues (let’s call him A) in the office for more than three hours before going home.

We talked about many things — relationships, marriage, cars, houses, work communication, and even company development.

On my way home, I thought about the time when I was recruiting new members for our team.

---

### The Hiring Process

A few months ago, when we were hiring, I read through hundreds of resumes and interviewed about twenty Java developers.

Finally, by the end of July, I found two candidates who seemed suitable — one of them was A, and the other was B.

I arranged an online interview with A the next morning, and a face-to-face interview with B in the afternoon.

After the morning interview with A, I told my mentor and other teammates that I thought A was really good.

I said, “Let’s still meet B this afternoon, but honestly, A already meets our requirements. His communication and understanding skills are strong, and his technical ability is good too.”

That afternoon, B came for his interview.

From his resume, B actually seemed to be a better match for our needs — his past work experience was more closely related to our current projects.

But during the interview, he gave me the feeling that he didn’t know much about anything I asked.

In the technical part, I started with some very basic questions, but he couldn’t answer even one.

He told me he hadn’t started preparing for interviews yet.

However, I felt that someone with several years of Java experience should be able to handle at least basic questions, even without preparation. That gave me a bad impression.

When it came to business-related questions, his answers were also very weak — scattered and unclear.

Later, my mentor also talked with B face to face. His opinion was: “He could be trained and used, but you might need to spend a lot of time teaching him.”

I told my mentor that I wanted to invite A for an on-site interview instead, because I had more confidence in him.

The next day, A came for the in-person interview, passed it smoothly, and officially became my new colleague.

---

### Working with the Right Person

I think my first impression was quite accurate — from the interview, I already felt that A and I would get along well.

Indeed, after he joined, we became good colleagues very quickly.

There’s also another Android developer in our department.

I’ve known him for more than one year, but we never really had enjoyable conversations.

One reason is that he’s very quiet, and another is that his communication and understanding skills are quite poor.

Because of this, I’ve had many difficulties working with him.

A, on the other hand, is different.

We’re both introverted, but when we meet people who are on the same wavelength, it’s easy to have a pleasant talk.

He’s also full of positive energy and goes to the gym regularly.

Recently, I’ve wanted to start exercising more, and with his help, I’ve begun some real workouts.

I even plan to join the same gym as him and ask for some training advice.

---

### A Promising Colleague

Tonight, A mentioned that he has been with the company for two months and will soon reach his probation period.

He asked me about his salary after becoming a full-time employee.

I told him that I had already talked to our department head before the National Day holiday, and I would do my best to help him get the salary he expects.

I’ve also told my leaders that A is an excellent employee — strong in both technical and communication skills, a rare find.

I really hope the company gives him a good offer so that he can work happily.

As his team leader, I’ll also help communicate his request to another manager.

It’s my responsibility to support him and fight for what he deserves.

After A joined, my workload became much lighter.

Before, I was handling heavy development tasks for the second half of the year while also maintaining several subsystems.

Sometimes I felt completely exhausted.

But A’s technical skills are very strong — in some areas, even better than mine.

He quickly understands new business logic and can develop features efficiently after just a short explanation.

Originally, we planned to finish all the development work by mid-to-late November.

But now, with our current progress, we might complete everything by next week — much earlier than planned.

As a result, both of us have been feeling more relaxed lately, and we’ve been working fewer overtime hours.

---

### Finding Balance and Enjoyment

Recently, besides work, reading, and writing, I’ve also spent about an hour every day studying English — listening to audio, watching videos, and practicing speaking.

Since I’ve found things I truly enjoy doing, I’ve reduced my online and offline social activities.

I just want to spend more time on what really interests me.

Tonight, I originally planned to go home early, read, write something, and study languages.

But the chat with A was so enjoyable that I didn’t mind changing my plans.

Since joining this company last year, work has been easy, but I didn’t really connect with the Android developer.

And since my work doesn’t overlap much with other departments, I hadn’t had a pleasant workplace chat for over a year.

A makes me feel like he could be a real friend, not just a coworker.

After our long talk tonight, I truly felt I made the right choice when hiring him.

I’m very glad I chose A to be my teammate.
