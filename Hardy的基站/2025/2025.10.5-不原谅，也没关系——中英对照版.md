# 不原谅，也没关系

## It’s Okay Not to Forgive

有些畸形的疗愈学说喜欢强迫遭受过心理创伤的人否认其所受到的创伤，或者将其“最小化”。受此不合理观点的影响，遭受创伤者的内在批判者会更加剧烈地诋毁他们是如此的不宽容，对其造成更多伤害。

Some unhealthy healing ideas try to force people who have suffered psychological trauma to deny their pain, or to make it seem “not serious.”

Under this wrong belief, the inner critic of the traumatized person may attack them even more, saying they are not kind enough — which brings more harm.

可是真正的原谅，是需要经过有效哀悼和情绪治疗工作才能够获得的。如果不进行大量的情绪疗愈工作，再多的想法、再强的意图或信念都不能实现真正的原谅。

But true forgiveness can only happen after real emotional healing and mourning.

Without deep emotional work, no matter how hard we think or how strong our belief is, real forgiveness cannot happen.

我看过很多类似这样的话术：不管一个人受到多大的伤害，都应该快速原谅别人，因为不这样做，痛苦的也只是自己罢了。相比起一边承受过往的伤害、一边带着怨恨继续生活，提前抛却掉怨恨，似乎是最佳答案。

I have always seen sayings like this:

“No matter how badly someone hurts you, you should forgive them quickly. Otherwise, you are only hurting yourself.”

Compared with living with hatred, letting go of resentment seems like the better choice.

我很不喜欢这类强行阉割掉一个正常人该有的情绪的观点！

But I really dislike this kind of idea — it cuts off the emotions that a normal person should have!

我们有什么理由，在还没有得到充分疗愈的情况下，就提前代替过去承受伤害的那个自己，去原谅一切？

Why should we forgive everything before we have fully healed?

Why should we replace the hurt version of ourselves and say, “It’s okay,” when it is not yet okay?

前几个月，我跟一个许久不见的高中同学见面聊了会儿天，期间，我们聊到关于彼此成长经历的事情，她所表达的一些观点，对我挺有启发的。

A few months ago, I met an old high school friend.

We talked about our past and how we grew up.

Her thoughts gave me some new ideas.

之后，我感觉自己似乎也打开了一些心结，也在试图引导自己去原谅一些人和事。

After that, I felt like I had unlocked some of my inner pain and started to try forgiving some people and things from my past.

可过一段时间后，我发现自己似乎还没有达到可以完全放下一些事情的境界，因此暂停了“原谅”这个行为。而后，我对此感到有些困惑，难道之前是我的错觉，还是说我需要变得更加豁达一些？

But after some time, I realized I still couldn’t completely let go.

So I stopped trying to “forgive” for a while.

Then I felt confused — was I wrong before? Should I be more open-minded?

最近，我在看书和思考的过程中找到了答案：在疗愈自己的过程中，偶尔出现情绪闪回，是一件很正常且完全有理由被自己接纳的一件事情。

Recently, while reading and thinking, I found my answer:

When we are healing ourselves, it’s normal to have emotional flashbacks sometimes.

这并非是我们不够宽容的表现，而是我们在正视自己内心的真实感受，给予自己足够多的内在支持。

That doesn’t mean we are not kind or tolerant.

It means we are facing our true feelings honestly and giving ourselves enough inner support.

我们并不需要以“圣人”的标准来要求自己，而是需要允许自己愤怒、允许自己宣泄情绪、允许自己远离那些伤害我们的人和环境。

We don’t need to act like saints.We need to allow ourselves to feel angry, to release emotions, and to stay away from people or places that hurt us.

有时受到某些启发，突然有种如释重负的感觉，说明我们在疗愈自己的过程，取得了很大的进步。至于后续出现的反弹情绪，也完全不会否认掉之前取得进步的既定结果。

Sometimes we suddenly feel light and free after being inspired — that shows we have made progress in healing.

If we later feel sad or angry again, it doesn’t erase that progress. It’s just part of the journey.

我突然发现，在疗愈自我的过程中，我似乎错误地认为尽快完善自我、减少负面情绪，是我应该追求的唯一目标。因为这可以帮助我最大程度减少内耗，然后专注于追求我所想要的东西。

I suddenly realized that, in my healing process, I had thought that my only goal was to become perfect quickly and reduce negative emotions.

I believed that would help me use less energy on pain and focus more on what I want.

尽管这样的想法还比较薄弱，但它的的确确影响了我的一些思维方式，有时会让我走进一些误区当中。

Even though that idea is not very strong, it still influenced me and sometimes led me in the wrong direction.

实际上，在快速放下愤怒情绪以及带着愤怒情绪过着内耗的生活之间，还有另外一条路可以走，那就是：远离不好的人和环境，同时正视常人该有的愤怒情绪，在此基础上，为了更好地养育自己，去逐步完善自我，成为一个更好的人！

In fact, between the two extremes — “letting go quickly” and “living with endless anger” — there is another way:

We can stay away from unhealthy people and environments, accept our normal human anger, and grow slowly into a better version of ourselves.

对于遭受创伤者来说，如果有一天，他/她真的看开了，能够打从心底里去原谅以前的人和事，那固然是件好事。

For people who have been hurt, if one day they truly forgive others from their hearts, that’s wonderful.

但是，要记住：不原谅，也没关系。

But remember this truth:

It’s okay not to forgive.

不原谅，你也可以成为你喜欢的那一类人。

Even if you don’t forgive,

you can still become the kind of person you want to be.

不原谅，你也依然可以过上你想要的生活。

Even if you don’t forgive,

you can still live the life you want.
